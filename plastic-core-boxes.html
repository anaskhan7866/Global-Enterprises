Key Advantages of Polymer Plastic Core Trays

Weight Reduction

60% Transport Freight Savings

Weighing merely ~2.4 kg per tray, polymer boxes drastically reduce payload weight for helicopter transport, fly-in camps, and long-distance freight to analytical assay laboratories.

Non-Magnetic

Geophysical Logging Friendly

Being 100% non-metallic, plastic trays produce zero magnetic interference, permitting core scanning with handheld magnetic susceptibility meters and pXRF devices directly in the box.

Chemical Resistance

Acid & Saline Proof

Impervious to acid mine drainage, sulfide-oxidizing core waters, hydrochloric acid testing drops, and coastal salt-spray environments that degrade ordinary metals.
