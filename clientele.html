4-Stage Quality Assurance Workflow

Our multi-checkpoint inspection ensures zero variance before dispatch to field drilling sites.

01

Coil Thickness & GSM

Digital micrometer gauge audit and Elcometer zinc coating checks on incoming Tata/Jindal coils.

02

CNC Channel Precision

Accurate hydraulic die bending for BQ, NQ, HQ, PQ channels preventing core movement and friction damage.

03

Perforation & Riveting
